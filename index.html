The science

The photosynthesis equation.

With light energy, plants turn carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.

Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen

Light energy powers the process. The balanced chemical equation is: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂.
